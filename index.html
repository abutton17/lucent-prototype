L2
Somatic regulation — first intervention, before any cognitive work
ScienceAge 0–3 trauma organizes at brainstem and early limbic level, before language develops. The nervous system learned dysregulation before it learned words. Body-based regulation is the only accessible entry point at this developmental level.
ActionBegin every session with 5-min 5-4-3-2-1 grounding. Teach 5.5/5.5 paced breathing. Assign 10-min daily body scan (Palouse Mindfulness, free). No traumatic content until somatic regulation is established.
